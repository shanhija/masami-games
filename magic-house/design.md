i want a game for roughly 7 years old kids with 1-4 players. each stage of a game is a room in a "magic house". a room has tasks and things to do, but those are designed by opus. also the room, its decor, objects, interactable things, all are designed by opus on the fly. 

the main thing is that 1-4 players figure out the task for the room, which need not be difficult to find out, and then try to do that. collaboration would be best, but competitions, or just parallel work are great as well. think of the game portal for 1 or 2 players, any platformer for any task, and anything else opus can think of. think of mario party mini games. the only requirement is that it takes like 3-5 minutes normally for a child to pass the game.

visually it uses the silly billy style, but is mostly from 2d viewpoint as a platformer. but some rooms may be 3d and extend beyond the normal size of the room. the building doesn't need to make sense.

while the kids are playing one room, opus is designing the next.

as a reward, there's perhaps some fireworks or some other celebrations, and each player gets a random (designed by opus) apparel. these can be from a predefined set of objects, and with a predefined set of colors or patterns. after each room, there's a dressing room where the players may update their appearance. 

perhaps there could be a free to use library of sounds opus could choose from, but make sure they are suitable for children. 

perhaps opus could get feedback from how to room goes for the kids and tune the difficulty based on that.

make the rooms have clearly different themes, like space, unicorns, cats, etc. don't give a clear list, and preferably don't give examples at all. look at how [dissertation ](https://github.com/shanhija/dissertation) instructs opus to create new things. i'm not sure it's required for this case for kids to always get a new room. they might want to revisit old ones. so keep a store of played rooms and allow the kids to redo them.

if the room is too difficult, add a restart button where they can restart. revisiting old rooms is also possible by the above.

the game should work both from keyboard and mouse, and from a tablet. note that it's ok for a tablet to limit to say 2 players max. consider the controls in this case. also note that the controls may change in each room, depending on what the room requires.
