# Masami games: instructions for Claude

Canonical project rules are the same as for Cursor: **`.cursor/rules/`** (plus root **`.cursorrules`** as a short Cursor pointer). **Read and follow those files** before substantive work; do not rely only on this summary.

**Load these paths** (principles; long examples stay in `docs/`):

- `.cursor/rules/maintain-canonical-rules.mdc`: keeping rules in sync; user corrections and redirects
- `.cursor/rules/coding-style.md`: languages, tooling, engineering defaults
- `.cursor/rules/plans.md`: where plan files live and how they evolve

Games for a five-year-old, one folder per game, all pages of the one Vite + React + TypeScript project at the repo root: the 3D ones on react-three-fiber, the 2D ones on the browser's canvas. The root `index.html` is the game directory: a tap on a card navigates to `<game>/index.html`, so only that game loads. Commands run at the root: `npm run dev` (port 3002), `npm run typecheck`, `npm test`, `npm run build`. `hosting/Dockerfile` builds the image that serves them (`README.md`, Hosting). The repo is public on GitHub: nothing of an employer's and no recording of the children goes in (`.cursor/rules/coding-style.md`, Public repo).

- **`chase/`**: the side-view cartoon police chase (see `chase/README.md`).
- **`foot-chase/`**: the two-player chase on foot (see `foot-chase/README.md`, brief in `foot-chase/design.md`).
- **`castle-builder/`**: the castle on a meadow island, seen from high up (see `castle-builder/README.md`, brief in `castle-builder/design.md`, decisions and open work in `plans/castle-builder.md`).
- **`pia-jump/`**: Pia Jump, a platformer that plays like Super Mario in true 2D on the canvas (see `pia-jump/README.md`, brief in `pia-jump/design.md`, decisions and open work in `plans/pia-jump.md`).
- **`rakennustyömaat/`**: Cat Hat Builder, drive a truck, make materials and build a house of themed rooms, side view on the canvas (see `rakennustyömaat/README.md`, brief in `rakennustyömaat/design.md`, decisions and open work in `plans/rakennustyömaat.md`). The folder keeps its Finnish name: URLs carry it percent-encoded (`rakennusty%C3%B6maat`).
- **`rocket-factory/`**: Rocket Factory, a kids' version of Factorio seen from straight above on the canvas: dig, place machine stickers, draw belts, make science, defend against the munchers, fill the rocket (see `rocket-factory/README.md`, brief in `rocket-factory/design.md`, decisions and open work in `plans/rocket-factory.md`).
- **`marienpeli/`**: Super Me, make your own person in a closet, then a platformer in Pia Jump's places where you build your way with planks, cubes, trampolines and ladders and find presents with new things to wear; it imports Pia Jump's physics constants, world art, camera, icons and synth (see `marienpeli/README.md`, brief in `marienpeli/design.md`, decisions and open work in `plans/marienpeli.md`). The folder keeps its Finnish name.
- **`limaukkopeli/`**: Slime Guy, a 3D obstacle course over water on react-three-fiber: steer, jump and hide under the spinners, grow on slime balls, carry the lost baby slimes, bounce up the tower past the numbered circles and open the surprise box for new clothes (see `limaukkopeli/README.md`, brief in `limaukkopeli/design.md`, decisions and open work in `plans/limaukkopeli.md`). The folder keeps its Finnish name.
- **`ruokapeli/`**: Monster Lunch, a course seen from above at a slant on the canvas: pick a burger or a croissant and its fillings, carry the plate round picking food off the ground past moving poles, water, colour gates and poop, then feed the dish from a hot air balloon to a monster on a rail track (see `ruokapeli/README.md`, the inventor's words in `ruokapeli/design.md`, decisions and open work in `plans/ruokapeli.md`). The folder keeps its Finnish name.
- **`silly-billy/`**: Silly Billy, 3D on react-three-fiber in Castle Builder's look: a room of avatars and things (a playground, a swimming pool, a forest); a child calls an avatar by name and says what to do, in Finnish or English by the pause card's setting, which every word shown, said and heard follows (the starting avatars are named in it); what is not known yet is understood and written as code by Opus through the Claude Code CLI from a dev-server plugin, tested in a sandbox and kept in `silly-billy/library/` for next time; avatars are made and dressed by voice too (see `silly-billy/README.md`, brief in `silly-billy/design.md`, decisions and open work in `plans/silly-billy.md`).

A new game starts as a short brief in Finnish at `<game>/design.md`, written by Sami. Treat it as the spec, mirror an existing game's `index.html` and `src/` (a game has no package.json, Vite config or tsconfig of its own), keep every audience-visible word in simple English for a five-year-old (a game with a language setting, like Silly Billy, shows, says and hears every word in the language chosen), and invent the details the brief leaves open ("keksi lisää" is an invitation). The look is each game's own: share another game's camera, palette and shading only when the brief names it ("kuten chase") or Sami asks, and then by importing its modules, never copying them. **Every new game is added to the directory**: a card on the root `index.html` and its folder name in `GAMES` in `vite.config.ts`.
